About Inavant

Built by engineers. Run by engineers.

We are a Louisiana-based control systems integrator delivering reliable, secure, and efficient automation solutions for industrial operations since 2016.

Our Story

Inavant was founded in 2016 because we saw where the industry was headed: a convergence point, where enterprise IT technology was going to move onto the plant floor. We set out to lead that integration — applying enterprise-class hardware, software, and security models to the automation world, and doing it right.

We also saw what was coming with it. The big vendors were positioning to lock their clients into proprietary platforms — licensed, subscription-bound, and closed. We bet the company on the opposite: open standards and open-source technology, applied with engineering discipline, could accomplish the same ends better and more securely. That conviction still drives what we build, nearly ten years on.

We started out promising reliable, secure automation solutions — and that promise still holds. But years of building, configuring, and maintaining these systems taught us what reliability and security actually deliver for our clients: uptime. Downtime is the Achilles heel of every industrial operation, and our systems measurably reduce it. So we've leaned in. That's the headline over everything we build now: Normalize Uptime.

Inavant is a licensed Professional Engineering firm in Louisiana (Firm #EF.0006374), with senior engineers who hold ISA/IEC 62443 credentials across the full cybersecurity lifecycle.

Why We Exist

Our vision is to improve the lives of the people who operate modern manufacturing facilities — building integrated automation solutions that move information off the plant floor and provide real insight into the heartbeat of the process.

We use current industrial technology, forward-looking cybersecurity standards, and automation best practices to lead in industrial automation and process IT — and we educate our clients on how to build and operate a reliable, secure 21st-century manufacturing environment.

Our Philosophy

Our focus is simple: reliable, secure control systems that stay flexible, OT infrastructure that's functional and protected, and data that's captured and visualized in ways that make sense for operations.

  • Senior engineers on every engagement
  • Extend and modernize what you have rather than replacing it unnecessarily
  • Experienced in both greenfield builds and brownfield modernization at operating facilities

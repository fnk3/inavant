Where We Work

Strategically located in the heart of the Louisiana manufacturing hub — and supporting clients across the country. Wherever the work is, we'll go there.

Specialty Chemicals

Batch and continuous process control, DCS tie-ins for unit additions, and the OT infrastructure behind safe, repeatable production.

Petroleum Refining

Control system upgrades and migrations, OT network modernization, and turnaround controls support for refineries large and small.

Marine, Truck & Rail Loading

Loading platform automation for truck, rail, and marine racks — metering, batching, and the data systems behind every load out the gate.

LNG & Midstream

Terminal automation, custody-critical data systems, and secure connectivity across distributed assets.

Pipelines

SCADA, station automation, and OT cybersecurity documented to satisfy TSA requirements.

Aggregates, Mining & Minerals

Sand, stone, ore, or any bulk material — crushing, washing, drying, and conveying automation for plants in remote locations, with the networking and remote access to match.

Pulp & Paper

Continuous process control and drives-heavy automation for mills that never get to stop.

Wood Products

PLC-driven automation for OSB, plywood, and lumber operations — line controls, scanners, and the networks behind them.

Power & Utilities

Generation and utility process control, secure SCADA, and infrastructure hardening for critical operations.

Water & Wastewater

Treatment plant automation, lift station SCADA, and compliance-ready reporting systems.

Food & Beverage

Production line automation, recipe management, and plant data systems with food-safety-conscious execution.

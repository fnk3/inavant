Greenfield Automation & OT Networking · Chemical Terminal · Houston, TX · 2020

Hydrochloric acid unloading & loading terminal

Situation
A new-build acid terminal needed a complete control system — railcar unloading, product storage and inventory management, blending and scrubbing, and truck trailer loading.
What we did
Designed and engineered an Allen-Bradley system (Studio 5000, FactoryTalk SE) with over 1,500 I/O points on 1756-L8 processors, layered to the full Purdue security model from the plant floor up to the IT/OT network. Produced all loop sheets, termination sheets, and cabinet schematics; designed and assembled every enclosure; terminated all instrument wiring. Built the full network infrastructure — redundant operator stations and SCADA servers, a network-based Motor Control Center with VFDs and smart overload starters integrated for diagnostics, and all fiber and copper terminations. Commissioned and started up the site with full loop and network verification, and installed the data historian.
Outcome
The terminal came online on schedule — and we still maintain and support the control system today.

Greenfield Automation & OT Networking · Aggregates & Mining · Shreveport, LA · 2022

Frac sand mining, cleaning & loading facility

Situation
A sand processing and load-out facility needed a control system built for a punishing environment where downtime isn't an option.
What we did
Designed and engineered an Allen-Bradley system (Studio 5000, Ignition HMI) with over 1,200 I/O points on 1756-L8 processors, layered to the full Purdue security model. Built the infrastructure for resilience: primary and secondary server racks at opposite ends of the facility — a quarter mile apart — connected in a fully redundant REP fiber ring, with an emergency wireless backhaul link as a final fallback against harsh conditions and human error. Designed and assembled all enclosures and network racks, terminated all instrument, fiber, and copper connections, commissioned the full system, and installed a data historian with reporting.
Outcome
A control system that keeps running even when the environment doesn't cooperate — and we still support it today.

Control System Modernization · Aggregates & Mining · Midland, TX · 2024

Obsolete PLC & SCADA replacement at an aggregate facility

Situation
An operating aggregate facility was running on an obsolete Modicon PLC and SCADA system — over 1,800 I/O points with no path forward on parts or support.
What we did
Evaluated the existing system and retro-designed a replacement on Allen-Bradley and Ignition, reusing the existing enclosures. Produced all new drawings for the retrofitted cabinets with complete field wire re-tagging, commissioned the new system with full loop and network verification, and installed a data historian with process reporting.
Outcome
A modern, supportable control system in the same footprint — and we still maintain it today.

OT Infrastructure · Thin Clients · Turnaround · Specialty Chemicals · Geismar, LA · 2025

Unit turnaround: SIS infrastructure upgrade & control room modernization

Situation
A virtualized Triconex safety instrumented system — infrastructure actively managed by Inavant — was due for a hardware refresh during a unit turnaround, and the aging operator HMI solution needed replacing.
What we did
Replaced the server hardware with a new Dell cluster, rebuilt the virtual machines on the latest Windows Server, and migrated and upgraded the control system software. Replaced the old firewalls with enterprise-class Cisco firewalls, working with plant IT on rules and traffic flow so all network traffic stays segregated and controlled — and coordinated with the DCS vendor to do the same on the DCS side. Rather than replace the HMI with something proprietary and closed off, we deployed our thin client management system to power the six-screen control room and the engineering workstation — any off-the-shelf PC can serve as a thin client, managed from a core VM.
Outcome
Modern, secure SIS infrastructure and a control room that's reliable, recoverable, and free of proprietary lock-in — all delivered inside the turnaround window.

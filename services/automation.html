What We Do

Automation & Process Control Engineering

DCS and PLC programming, configuration, and migration — built around your existing infrastructure and sized to what the application actually needs. Full build, modernization, or something in between.

What We Deliver

  • DCS & PLC programming and configuration — new logic, modifications, and optimization on running systems.
  • Control system migrations and upgrades — legacy platform modernization with minimal downtime, planned around your outage windows.
  • HMI development — intuitive, high-performance operator graphics that make sense to the people who run the unit.
  • Systems integration — tying new assets into existing infrastructure seamlessly; DCS tie-ins for packages and skids.
  • Process historians & data visualization — capturing operational data and getting the right data to the right people at the right time.
  • Panel fabrication — a full panel shop ready to build, wire, and test control panels for new builds and upgrades alike.

Platforms

We work across all major DCS, SCADA, and PLC platforms — including DeltaV, Rockwell, and Yokogawa — rather than locking you into proprietary systems. Your platform, your standards, our engineering.

Brownfield & Greenfield

We do new builds from the ground up — designed, configured, built, and tested fully in-house — all the way up to full modernizations at operating facilities. And where it makes sense, we take a third path: instead of scrapping a system that's still running, we make smart, targeted upgrades — a portion here, a tweak there — that add years of life to an older system at a fraction of the cost of full replacement.

What We Do

Long-Term Support

The project ends. The system runs for twenty years. We stay.

What We Deliver

  • Support agreements — defined response times and a team that already knows your system.
  • Routine logic & HMI changes — added a piece of equipment out in the plant? We handle the small changes for you, without making them a project.
  • Training — we train your E&I techs, automation techs, and operators on the equipment they operate, because we want your people to be successful too.
  • SOPs & change management — we create and maintain SOPs and management-of-change procedures where they don't exist.
  • Active monitoring & program backups — cabinets, controllers, and configurations monitored, backed up, and verified.
  • OT infrastructure care — servers, virtual machines, backups, and software updates kept functional and protected.
  • Patch management — OS and application patching tested and scheduled around operations, not against them.
  • Historian & data system support — keeping the data flowing and the dashboards meaningful.
  • Scheduled site visits — proactive health checks on the automation systems and the OT systems alike, instead of emergency calls.

Why It Matters

Most control system failures aren't dramatic — they're a backup that quietly stopped running, a server drive that filled up, an update that never got applied. Long-term support is about making sure no unexpected issue affects your operations.

Because we're a lean team of senior engineers, the person answering your support call is the same caliber of engineer who built the system — often the same person. Our intent is simple: one number to call, a single point of contact for your control system and the OT infrastructure behind it.

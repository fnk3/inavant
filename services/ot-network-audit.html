OT Network Audit

A clear picture of your plant network — what's on it, what's at risk, and exactly what to do about it. Read-only and passive, with no impact to running operations.

The Network Nobody Drew

Most plant networks weren't designed — they accumulated. Twenty years of projects, contractors, turnover, and quick fixes leave behind flat networks, unmanaged switches in field panels, temporary remote access that became permanent, and drawings that no longer match what's installed. With no cohesive design or standard to build to, installs get made to the preference of whoever is on site that day, and troubleshooting drags on because no one has accurate drawings or a true inventory.

Most networks run on implicit trust — they work, but no one is actively segmenting, monitoring, or controlling what gets plugged in. It all runs fine, until a failure, an incident, or an insurance questionnaire forces the question: what's actually out there? You can't protect what you can't see.

What the Audit Covers

A current-state evaluation goes deep — these are the questions we answer about your system, graded against the Purdue Model, NIST SP 800-82, and ISA/IEC 62443.

Automation Systems

Platform fit for current and future needs. Hardware lifecycle, firmware, and spare capacity. Recommended spares — and whether they're actually on the shelf. Cabinet health: wiring, circuit protection, organization, documentation. License currency and ownership. Logic quality and offline backups.

Network & Infrastructure

Server age, warranty, and redundancy. Hypervisor and OS currency, security updates, version management. Monitoring and alerting — who gets alerted, and what's not alerting at all. Disaster recovery: on-site and off-site backups. Secure remote access and access control — every way in from the outside, including the ones nobody remembers setting up.

Segmentation & Traffic

VLAN design and native-VLAN hygiene. Daisy-chained switches vs. a hierarchical distribution layer. Spanning tree and root-bridge configuration. IT VLANs leaking into OT over shared trunks. Port-level health — duplex mismatches, errors, and collisions. East-west traffic visibility and the embedded switches inside your control and SCADA platforms — the layer most audits skip.

Process & Governance

SOPs and management-of-change procedures — and creating them where they don't exist. The IT/OT delineation point and a working relationship with your IT group. Lifecycle management, vendor contracts, and emergency support readiness. Single points of failure: aging hardware, end-of-life operating systems, and the one switch that takes the unit down with it.

How It Works

1 · Walk-Down & Collection

We look at everything that touches the network: an on-site survey of panels, racks, and rooms, plus passive, read-only data gathering. No production impact.

2 · Analysis & Drawings

We map what we found into current-state drawings and grade findings against the Purdue Model, NIST SP 800-82, and ISA/IEC 62443.

3 · Findings & Roadmap

Sit-down review of risk-ranked findings and a prioritized remediation plan, from quick wins through capital items.

What You Walk Away With

  • Current-state network drawings of the OT environment.
  • Risk-ranked findings report graded to the Purdue Model, NIST SP 800-82, and ISA/IEC 62443 — written in plain language for both plant and corporate audiences.
  • Hardware lifecycle disposition table — every device with its end-of-sale and end-of-support status and a replacement call.
  • Configuration baselines for the equipment we touch.
  • Prioritized remediation roadmap separating the fixes you can make next week from the ones that belong in next year's budget.

Everything is yours to keep — usable with any vendor, in any insurance or compliance conversation.

OT & SCADA Master Planning

Before you spend a dollar on upgrades, you need a map. A master plan evaluates your goals, your systems, and your existing infrastructure to create a sustainable path forward — with reliability, security, uptime, and cost savings as the forefront considerations.

It starts with knowing what's installed, where it is, and what its health is — and getting to know the people who run it, not just the systems. From there we get everything working at some level before tackling upgrades, then work from the small issues up to the larger projects, prioritized with your stakeholders and with uptime as the requirement.

And we treat your money like our own: where we find ways to improve operational efficiency and cut operating costs, those items take priority — so the savings can fund the larger initiatives.

  • Identify every piece of hardware, software, and communications installed
  • Evaluate each component as part of the overall architecture
  • Recommend changes where it makes sense — and fortify the good pieces already in operation
  • Act as a single point of contact for your SCADA and OT infrastructure

What Our Assessment Covers

A current-state evaluation goes deep — these are the questions we answer about your system.

Automation Systems

Platform fit for current and future needs. Hardware lifecycle, firmware, and spare capacity. Recommended spares — and whether they're actually on the shelf. Cabinet health: wiring, circuit protection, organization, documentation. License currency and ownership. Logic quality and offline backups.

Network & Infrastructure

Server age, warranty, and redundancy. Hypervisor and OS currency, security updates, version management. Monitoring and alerting — who gets alerted, and what's not alerting at all. Disaster recovery: on-site and off-site backups. Secure remote access and access control.

Process & Governance

SOPs and management-of-change procedures — and creating them where they don't exist. The IT/OT delineation point and a working relationship with your IT group. Lifecycle management, vendor contracts, and emergency support readiness.

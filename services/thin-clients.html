What Beacon Is

One platform network-boots and manages every operator screen in your facility — control room consoles, loading racks, kiosks, and office terminals. Beacon, by Inavant, is built on a hardened open-source foundation (Debian Linux + LTSP) with Inavant's management console for content placement, per-monitor assignment, and instant client replacement. Deployed as a redundant server pair with active failover — lose a server and the operator never notices.

It's a drop-in replacement for ThinManager® installations — without the proprietary lock-in.

  • Open foundation — a Debian + LTSP open-source core, with Inavant's custom management console built on top. No black-box platform: the foundation is inspectable, patchable, and auditable by your security team.
  • Any hardware — we have recommended hardware, but you're never locked into it. Any PXE-capable PC from any OEM, driving any monitor. Stock ordinary spares on the shelf.
  • Stateless clients — nothing lives on the edge device. A failed terminal is a swap, not a rebuild.
  • No lock-in — you own the hardware and the platform. No compatibility lists, no activation servers, and no vendor can buy it out from under you or change the terms.
  • Any HMI — Ignition, Wonderware, FactoryTalk® — over RDP, in a browser, or Linux-native.
  • One team — deployed and supported by the firm already handling your controls, OT network, and cybersecurity.

Why Open

Proprietary thin-client platforms decide what hardware you can buy, when your version expires, and what changes after the next acquisition. Their inner workings are closed — your engineers can't inspect them, and your security team can't audit them. When a terminal dies, getting it back means tickets and waiting.

Beacon takes the opposite approach: we use open-source software as the foundation — hardened and configured with engineering discipline — and build our own custom management tools on top of it, purpose-built for the industrial application. You can see exactly how the foundation works, stock ordinary hardware as spares, and recover on your own terms. Openness isn't a philosophy — it's what makes the system more reliable and more secure.

Proven in Production

Beacon isn't a concept — it runs today at industrial sites from the Gulf South to the Midwest, including control rooms in operating chemical plants and aggregate processing facilities that run it exclusively: industrial control rooms, aggregate processing & loading, truck and rail loading platforms, operator kiosks, and hub-and-satellite operations.

The Security Dividend

Thin client architecture is an OT security win: no applications or data at the edge, one centrally patched server to manage dozens of plant-floor PCs, and stateless devices wiped clean on every boot. Backed by Inavant's industry-leading OT cybersecurity practice, with ISA/IEC 62443 cybersecurity expertise on staff.

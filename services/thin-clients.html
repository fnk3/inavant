What Beacon Is

One platform network-boots and manages every operator screen in your facility — control room consoles, loading racks, kiosks, and office terminals. Beacon is built on a hardened open-source foundation (Debian Linux + LTSP) with Inavant's management console for content placement, per-monitor assignment, and instant client replacement. Deployed as a redundant server pair with active failover — lose a server and the operator never notices.

It's a drop-in replacement for ThinManager® installations — without the licensing.

  • Any hardware — we have recommended hardware, but you're never locked into it. Any PXE-capable PC from any OEM, driving any monitor.
  • Stateless clients — nothing lives on the edge device. A failed terminal is a swap, not a rebuild.
  • Zero license fees — no per-seat licenses, no subscriptions, no activation servers. Ever.
  • No lock-in — open stack, top to bottom. You own your hardware. No vendor can buy it out from under you or change the terms.
  • Any HMI — Ignition, Wonderware, FactoryTalk® — over RDP, in a browser, or Linux-native.
  • One team — deployed and supported by the firm already handling your controls, OT network, and cybersecurity.

The Screen Tax

Centralized thin client management transformed the plant floor — then the licensing changed. Today the leading commercial platform attaches a recurring cost to every screen, forever: per-connection licenses, annual subscription or maintenance fees, and a hardware compatibility program that limits what you can buy and stock. When a terminal dies, getting it back means tickets, licensing, and waiting.

Beacon ends that. You pay for engineering and support. You never pay for a license.

Proven in Production

Beacon isn't a concept — it runs today at industrial sites from the Gulf South to the Midwest, including control rooms in operating chemical plants and aggregate processing facilities that run it exclusively: industrial control rooms, aggregate processing & loading, truck and rail loading platforms, operator kiosks, and hub-and-satellite operations.

The Security Dividend

Thin client architecture is an OT security win: no applications or data at the edge, one centrally patched server to manage dozens of plant-floor PCs, and stateless devices wiped clean on every boot. Backed by Inavant's industry-leading OT cybersecurity practice, with ISA/IEC 62443 cybersecurity expertise on staff.

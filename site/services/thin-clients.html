What We Do

Thin Client Management

Operator workstations that are easier to secure, manage, and recover — without a PC under every desk.

What We Deliver

  • Thin client architecture & deployment — centralized session delivery for operator and engineering workstations.
  • Centralized management — one place to control what every station can see and do, by role and by location.
  • Fast recovery — a failed station is a hardware swap, not a rebuild. Minutes, not days.
  • Security by design — no local data, controlled USB access, and tighter audit control across the control room.
Confirm with the team: platform specifics (e.g., ThinManager / other), typical deployment sizes, any reference setups worth describing.

Why It Matters

Traditional operator PCs accumulate risk — local configurations, unpatched software, and one-off builds that nobody remembers how to restore. A managed thin client environment replaces that with sessions delivered from secured, redundant servers.

For operations, that means consistent workstations everywhere. For IT/OT, it means patching and securing one environment instead of fifty machines.
